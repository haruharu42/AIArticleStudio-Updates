AI Article Studio v0.4.2.4
================================

This update improves the article-linked image workflow.

- Moves image planning before completed-article creation.
- Splits image selection into independent eyecatch and illustration checkboxes.
- Defaults to eyecatch ON and article illustrations OFF.
- Adds AI-selected illustration count with a safe maximum of six.
- Keeps manual illustration counts from one through six.
- Adds illustration markers to the completed-article prompt only when illustrations are enabled.
- Generates eyecatch and illustration prompts from the completed article.

The updater verifies v0.4.2.3 before patching and validates the installation after applying the update.
