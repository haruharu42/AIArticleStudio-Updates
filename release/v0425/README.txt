AI Article Studio v0.4.2.5
================================

This update streamlines article creation and protects Web AI drafts.

- Converts the long creation form into short sequential setup pages.
- Shows one Web AI production step at a time with Back and Next controls.
- Adds a right-side recent-work panel with up to ten local drafts.
- Saves the current draft before switching history or starting a new article.
- Adds a dedicated button to clear only the pasted/final article fields.
- Separates starting a new article from clearing the current pasted text.
- Restores selected title, pasted AI output, formatted article, step, and article settings from history.
- Keeps history data on the local PC under AIArticleStudio data.

The updater verifies v0.4.2.4 before patching and validates the installation after applying the update.
