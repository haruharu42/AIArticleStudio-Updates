AI Article Studio v0.4.2.7
================================

This update replaces the legacy stacked article form with the dedicated visual wizard approved in the UI mockups.

- Uses a consistent six-step progress bar: generation, image plan, basic settings, article design, creation, completion.
- Shows one focused screen at a time without opening a separate application window.
- Keeps Back and Next actions fixed and preserves entered settings while moving between steps.
- Separates eyecatch and illustration choices; AI can choose only the illustrations the article actually needs.
- Clears the Web AI paste area explicitly and restores earlier drafts only from recent history.
- Keeps up to ten recent local work items.
- Shows a decorated article preview on completion.
- Shows eyecatch and illustration prompts with their proposed insertion positions in the completion screen.

The updater verifies canonical v0.4.2.6 before making changes and validates the installed UI module after patching.
